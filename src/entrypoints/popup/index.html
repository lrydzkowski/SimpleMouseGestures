Managing gestures

A gesture is a sequence of mouse moves: U is up, R is right, D is down, L is left.

  • To add a gesture, type its letters in the Gesture field (or press the arrow keys), pick an operation, and click Add or press Enter.
  • To modify a gesture, delete its row and add the gesture again with the new operation.
  • To delete a gesture, click the trash icon in its row.

A gesture can have at most 8 moves and cannot repeat the same move twice in a row.

Replace all settings?

All current gestures and settings will be replaced. You can use Export first to keep a copy.
